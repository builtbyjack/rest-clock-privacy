Rest Clock Privacy Policy

Effective October 14, 2026.

Rest Clock is developed by builtbyjack. This policy describes the native Android app; it does not describe third-party websites, Google Play, or email providers.

Data on your device

Rest Clock stores your selected rest duration, sound and display preferences, and timer state in the app's private storage on your device so that the timer and saved preferences work. It does not ask for a user account or access your contacts, location, microphone, photos, or health records.

Collection and sharing

The current app does not send your timer settings or usage data to builtbyjack or other companies. It contains no advertising, analytics, account system, or third-party tracking SDK. It does not request Android's Internet permission. Android and Google Play operate under their own privacy policies.

Permissions

Notification permission enables rest-completion notifications. Alarms and reminders access allows Android to schedule completion alerts. Vibration is used for completion feedback. These permissions support the timer; they do not provide personal information to builtbyjack.

Retention and deletion

Preferences and timer state remain in app storage until replaced, the app's storage is cleared in Android settings, or the app is uninstalled. Returning to Ready stops the workout but retains your settings. The app disables Android cloud backup. Device-to-device migration behavior can depend on Android/device implementation.

Support messages

If you email support, your email address and the contents of your message are received by builtbyjack and the email provider to respond to your request. This is separate from automatic app data collection. Do not include sensitive information that is not needed to resolve your issue.

Contact

For questions about this policy or support correspondence, contact: builtbyjack.support@gmail.com.

Changes

If app features or data practices change, this policy will be updated before those changes are released.
